# Changelog / 更新日志

## 1.1.0 — 2026-10-24

### English

- Give individual workspaces their own persona name, preset and style guidance while keeping shared defaults elsewhere.
- Choose workspace or conversation independence directly beside the conversation input, including before the first message.
- Saved persona settings apply to subsequent responses immediately. Independent learning stays in its selected profile and does not update the global persona.
- Optionally link dsh-bili-scroll to retain video notes in one workspace and include its persona and content memory in later runs. Existing comment and messaging choices are preserved.
- Refocused the user guide on workspace styles and persona assignments; Bilibili companions are an optional integration.

### 中文

- 为指定工作区单独设置人格名称、预设和风格要求，其余工作区仍可沿用共享默认设置。
- 对话输入框旁新增人格入口，新建或已有对话均可选择工作区独立或仅当前对话独立。
- 保存后后续回复即时采用新设置，独立学习只更新当前档案，不回写全局人格。
- 可选关联刷 B 站插件，将视频笔记保存在一个工作区，并在后续任务中使用该人格与内容记忆；保留已有评论和私信设置。
- 使用说明重新以工作区风格与人格分配为主线，大肥鱼作为附加联动功能。

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-24

### English

**Give your assistant a personality and a style that grows with you.**

- Added companion persona and adaptive style modes: choose from five presets, or explore a style that gradually adapts to your feedback.
- Added background style learning to refine tone, detail and interaction using your explicit conversation feedback.
- Added user habits: keep communication preferences in one editable summary and approve new suggestions before they take effect.
- Added independent scopes for persona and habits: share everywhere, within a workspace, or keep each conversation separate.
- Refreshed settings with consistent animations, readable dark/light menus and complete Chinese/English interfaces.
- Existing role-based model routing and project experience remain available. New persona and habits profiles are optional and off by default.

### 中文

**给你的助手一点性格，让每次对话更合拍。**

- 新增陪伴人格与自适应风格模式：五种预设可选，也可以自主探索，逐渐找到适合你的相处方式。
- 支持后台自主风格学习，根据你的明确对话反馈调整语气、详略与互动方式。
- 新增用户习惯：用一段可编辑摘要管理交流偏好，新的建议经你确认后生效。
- 人格与习惯分别支持全局共享、工作区独立、对话独立。
- 更新设置界面：统一动效，改善深浅主题菜单可读性，补齐中英文体验。
- 保留原有角色模型路由与项目经验；新的人格和习惯档案默认关闭，按需开启。
